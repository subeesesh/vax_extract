Notes
- pets[]: one entry per pet on the document (multi-pet invoices give several), each with its own vaccinations.
- vaccinations[]: one entry per requested vaccine name, in request order. Printed vaccines nobody asked for are left out.
- name is the requested name. dateAdministered / expirationDate are those of the latest dose; the expiry may be
  worked out from a printed interval ('3 Year') when no expiry date is printed. A combination expires with its
  first-expiring part.
- matchedComponents: every printed row matched to that name, newest first. name and dates exactly as on the document;
  a date the document does not print is left out (never calculated).
- Requested but not on the document: dateAdministered and expirationDate are null, matchedComponents is [].
- partialMatch / needsHumanReview appear only when true (partialMatch: the printed rows cover only part of the name).
- Empty pet / owner / clinic / veterinarian fields are left out. There is no confidence score.
